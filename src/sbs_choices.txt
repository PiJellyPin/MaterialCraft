# What the numbered settings ("dropdowns") of the standard library graphs mean in MaterialCraft: one line per
# setting, `<graph id> <parameter> = <label of 0>|<label of 1>|...`. The labels say what MaterialCraft draws for each
# number (how sbs_adapt.rs reads it), so they are our own reading, not text taken from the library.
#   <graph id> may be `package/graph`, may end with `*` (every graph whose id starts with the rest) or be `*` alone
#   (every graph); a line naming the package wins over one that does not;
#   a label `N:text` gives the number explicitly (numbers that are left out are unused);
#   labels marked "(assumed)" are not yet checked against a reference render.
